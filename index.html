About

Storytelling dictates the language.

I've worked as a cinematographer for more than 20 years, across international drama, award-winning festival films and premium commercial work. I don't carry a fixed style from job to job. Each script tells me what the image needs to be.

Directors tend to describe me as a steady hand with an open, experimental eye. Clear communication and structure on set, and room to try things. I'm rooted in traditional craft, and I use new tools where they earn their place, such as AI-driven previsualisation that lets us test ideas long before the camera rolls.

I run Nordic Light Cinematography AB from Stockholm and work from Gothenburg as well, on productions in Sweden and abroad. I'm a member of FSF, the Swedish Society of Cinematographers.
