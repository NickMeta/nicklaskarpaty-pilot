Cinematography Without Compromise: How to Thrive in Tight Deadlines

By Nicklas Karpaty, FSF · Published 5 November 2023 · 2 min read

Hey fellow film makers! Let's talk about a really important topic: deadlines. We've all been there—racing against the clock to get that perfect shot before the sun sets or rushing to wrap up a project before the client's deadline. It's enough to make even the most seasoned filmmaker break a sweat. I just want to share some tips on how you can cope under pressure and still deliver as planned, no matter how tight the deadline.

As a cinematographer, I've learned a thing or two about working efficiently without sacrificing quality. It has much to do about staying organised, staying focused, and staying calm under pressure. Easier said than done, right? But with the right mindset and a few tricks up your sleeve, you can conquer even the tightest deadlines.

Firstly, it's all about planning ahead. I work closely with directors, first ADs and producers to map out a detailed shooting schedule, leaving no room for guesswork or last-minute surprises. (There are many great tools for us to use. I will dig into this closer in a future blog post for sure. ) By anticipating potential challenges and having contingency plans in place, we can stay one step ahead and keep the production on track.

But sometimes, despite our best efforts, things don't always go according to plan. That's where adaptability comes into play. As a cinematographer with a documentary film background, I've learned to think on my feet and make quick decisions in the heat of the moment. Whether it's adjusting camera settings on the fly or finding creative workarounds for unexpected obstacles, flexibility is key to staying ahead of the game.

Of course, none of this would be possible without a rock-solid team behind me. Collaboration is essential when working under tight deadlines, and I'm grateful to have worked with some truly talented crews and individuals who share my passion for storytelling. Together, we can overcome any challenge and deliver work that exceeds expectations—no matter how tight the deadline. So, here's to embracing the thrill of the deadline and proving that quality and efficiency can go hand in hand. With the right mindset and a sprinkle of creativity, there's nothing we can't achieve.

During my film school years, Swedish director Kjell Grede, once said to me, 'Never start with a compromise.' I still try to stick with these words of wisdom.
